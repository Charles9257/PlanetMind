# PlanetMind Investor and Advisor One-Pager

## What PlanetMind Is
PlanetMind is a risk intelligence platform that helps institutions anticipate and mitigate cascading global risks (climate, health, cyber, and economic) before they become large-scale crises.

## Why Now
- Global risks are increasingly connected and harder to manage in silos
- Organizations need faster decisions grounded in real-time evidence
- Existing systems are fragmented, reactive, and difficult to scale across domains

## The Problem
Decision-makers currently rely on disconnected tools and delayed analysis. This creates blind spots in cross-domain risk propagation and slows intervention planning when time is critical.

## Our Solution
PlanetMind unifies data ingestion, model-driven prediction, and scenario simulation in one decision-support workspace.

### Today (MVP)
- Climate model training and prediction workflows
- Model versioning and promotion controls
- Persistent prediction history for auditability
- Web-based stakeholder dashboard

### Next
- Health and cyber modules
- Cross-domain risk timeline and correlation insights
- Digital twin scenarios for intervention planning

## Differentiation
- Cross-domain design from day one (not a single-domain bolt-on)
- Governance-ready model lifecycle (versioning + promotion controls)
- Explainability-first strategy for trust in high-stakes use cases
- Modular architecture that supports enterprise and public-sector deployment paths

## Target Customers
