System overview

A Cloud-Native Federated, Explainable Multi-Agent AI Platform for Predicting, Simulating, and Mitigating Global Risks

Anticipate before escalation

  • Fuses satellite, sensor, health, economic and open-source signals.
  • Early-warning forecasts with calibrated uncertainty.
  • Federated learning keeps sensitive data where it lives.

Explore what-if futures

  • Agent-based and physical models run as coupled scenarios.
  • Stress-test cascading effects across domains.
  • Compare policy options side by side.

Turn insight into action

  • Ranked, costed intervention playbooks.
  • Human-in-the-loop approval and audit trails.
  • Feedback from outcomes retrains every agent.
